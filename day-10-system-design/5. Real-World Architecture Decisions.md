# Real-World Architecture Decisions

These are published engineering case studies for specific products and periods. The lessons are design takeaways from those cases.

| Company | Pressure and decision | What the case helps explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uber | As dependencies among services became hard to manage, Uber grouped related microservices into domains and gave those domains clearer entry points through gateways. | Independent deployment still needs clear rules about how teams and services depend on each other. |
| Grab | Grab's matching research treated a window of rider requests and drivers as an assignment problem. It explored how to avoid computing unnecessary exact travel costs while still finding optimal assignments in the studied setup. | Batching can improve the choice of assignments. The matching window and the cost of computing candidates affect how quickly riders get an answer. |
| Shopify | Shopify kept its core Rails application as a single deployable application while introducing owned components and tools to enforce dependency boundaries. | A modular monolith can improve ownership and code boundaries without taking on network calls between those modules. |
| Discord | Discord moved its message storage from Cassandra to ScyllaDB and added a data service that could coalesce repeated reads. Its channel-and-time-bucket model still had hot partitions for busy channels. | Database choice, partition keys, and the traffic sent to those partitions all affect latency. |
| Segment | Segment combined many similar server-side destination services into a shared delivery service after deployment and dependency maintenance became costly. Its routing design still had to contain failures at slow destinations. | Combine closely related work when separate deployments add overhead, and preserve the failure boundaries that matter. |
| Prime Video | The audio and video quality monitoring team moved tightly coupled processing into one process to avoid orchestration and intermediate data transfer costs. The team reported a 90% cost reduction for that service. | A network boundary can be expensive for stages that exchange large intermediate data. The reported result concerns the monitoring workload. |
