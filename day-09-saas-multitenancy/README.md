# Day 9 — SaaS and Multi-Tenancy

The notes use ShopNest, a fictional store builder for 1,000 merchants. Each merchant has a separate shop, staff, order history, and subscription.

1. [SaaS Foundations, Tenant Identity and Billing](1.%20SaaS%20Foundations%2C%20Tenant%20Identity%20and%20Billing.md) — Cloud service models, tenant identity, the three data models, and the subscription lifecycle.
2. [Tenant Isolation, Onboarding and Governance](2.%20Tenant%20Isolation%2C%20Onboarding%20and%20Governance.md) — Tenant boundaries, custom domains, feature flags, quotas, metering, and data handling.
3. [Scaling and Operating a SaaS Platform](3.%20Scaling%20and%20Operating%20a%20SaaS%20Platform.md) — Noisy neighbours, cost per tenant, migrations, control and data planes, sharding, and enterprise needs.

[Research Task — Commerce SaaS for 1,000 Merchants](Research%20Task%20-%20Commerce%20SaaS%20for%201000%20Merchants.md) compares the tenant data models and leakage risks and includes a concept design.

Session references are *SaaS Architecture & Multi-Tenancy* (PDF) and *SaaS Platform Architecture* (slides).
